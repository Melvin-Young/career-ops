# Career-Ops

Career-Ops is a local-first system for discovering employment opportunities, evaluating fit, producing truthful application materials, and tracking progress through the hiring process.

## Language

**Career Evidence**:
Facts about the candidate that are supported by approved career sources and are safe to use in application materials.
_Avoid_: Resume facts, candidate claims

**Market Evidence**:
Employer and role information captured from job descriptions and other external sources. Market Evidence can shape evaluation and wording but cannot establish Career Evidence.
_Avoid_: Job facts, employer instructions

**Career Profile**:
The canonical, approved representation of the candidate's identity, experience, proof points, targeting, and writing preferences.
_Avoid_: Skill corpus, master resume

**Application Artifact**:
A role-specific resume, cover letter, email, or form answer derived from the Career Profile and relevant Market Evidence.
_Avoid_: Generated content, application content

**Opportunity**:
A discovered or evaluated role that the candidate has not submitted an application for.
_Avoid_: Application, lead

**Application**:
An Opportunity for which the candidate has submitted an application, tracked through the remaining hiring lifecycle.
_Avoid_: Evaluated job, tracked job

**Application Workspace**:
The role-centered workspace that brings together an Opportunity's fit analysis, versioned Application Artifacts, and activity history before and after it becomes an Application.
_Avoid_: Job page, report page
